What is a commercial real estate loan?

A commercial real estate loan finances income-producing or business property: apartment buildings, retail centers, offices, warehouses, self-storage and more. A commercial bridge loan is short-term money to acquire, reposition or refinance a property while its income or condition is in transition. A commercial DSCR loan is long-term financing for a stabilized property, qualified on the income the property produces rather than on your personal income.

Many borrowers use both: a bridge loan to buy and reposition, then a DSCR loan once the property is stabilized. It's the same playbook as our residential bridge-to-DSCR path, applied to commercial property.

Commercial FAQ

Common questions about commercial loans.

What commercial loans does SLA Capital offer?

Two products. Commercial Bridge Loans up to $20,000,000: short-term financing to buy, reposition or refinance a commercial property. Commercial DSCR Loans up to $3,000,000: long-term financing for stabilized commercial property, qualified on the property's income.

What property types do you finance?

Multi-family, mixed-use, retail and strip centers, office, light industrial, warehouse, self-storage, mobile home parks, commercial condos, automotive, restaurants and bars, day care, and bulk residential.

Do you lend on owner-occupied commercial property?

Yes. Both commercial products are available to investors and to owner-occupied commercial borrowers: a business buying or refinancing the property it operates from. These are business-purpose loans; we don't make consumer home loans.

What terms are available on a commercial DSCR loan?

A 5-year hybrid, or a 15, 25 or 30-year fixed term, with full doc, lite doc, bank statement and no-doc documentation options.

What are your commercial loan rates?

Commercial loans are reviewed and priced case by case, so there's no published rate. Send the scenario and a loan officer comes back with terms for your deal.

Can I refinance or take cash out?

Yes. Both commercial products cover purchases, refinances and cash-out refinances.

How do I apply?

Use the commercial application. Choose bridge or DSCR, tell us about the property and the deal, and an SLA loan officer follows up.
