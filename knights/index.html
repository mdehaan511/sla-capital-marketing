Questions

Who is the lender?

SLA Capital (Sir Lends A Lot LLC, NMLS ID #2863552). Underwriting, closing, funding, servicing and the lending compliance sit with SLA.

What do I do?

Introduce investors and share your link and materials. You never quote, negotiate or discuss rates or terms, and you never take an application or a borrower's documents.

How do I earn?

A revenue share on the first loan each investor you bring funds with SLA, at your rank's rate. Plus a one-time $250 bonus when someone you recruit funds their first referred loan, and contest prizes. Paid monthly through Bill.com once your W-9 and agreement are on file. Nothing is owed on a loan that does not fund.

Do I need a lending license?

Most partners do not, because a partner never quotes, negotiates or arranges a loan; SLA is the licensed lender. Some states set their own rules for paying referral partners, and the partner agreement explains how SLA handles that.

What do I have to tell people?

That you are paid for referrals. Every post in your kit already starts with "#ad · paid SLA Capital referral partner".

What kinds of loans?

Business-purpose loans for real estate investors: fix-and-flip and bridge, new construction, and rental (DSCR) loans, including small multifamily. No owner-occupied home loans.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No fee to join and nothing to buy.

Where does SLA lend?

Nationwide except AZ, CA, MN, ND, NV, SD, UT, VT and US territories. Fix & Flip and New Construction also exclude IL, the city of Newark NJ, and rural properties. Multifamily 5+ also excludes ID.

How do I join?

Apply in about five minutes and sign the Knight Partner Agreement online. An SLA admin reviews every application, and once you're approved your link and portal arrive by email.
