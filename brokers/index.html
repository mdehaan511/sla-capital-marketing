What Preferred Partners get

Live pricing, on your own

Price deals 24/7 against SLA's current rate sheets: rate, points, loan amount, total fees and required liquidity, plus what you earn — before your borrower is under contract.

Every program, one lender

DSCR rentals (1–4 units and 5+), bridge and fix-and-flip, and ground-up construction.

A branded term sheet in one click

Download it, or send it to your borrower to e-sign from the portal.

Submit in one step

Send a priced deal straight to your SLA loan officer for approval. Ask for an exception on a submitted deal when it needs one.

Your Deals, live

Every loan's stage: received, approved, in processing, clear to close, funded. Upload documents for your borrower and work the file with SLA.

Your borrower is protected

A borrower you bring stays tied to you for 365 days from your last loan with them, even if they later come to SLA directly.

Send the application your way

Send the loan application with SLA branding, or without it on a neutral domain that shows your company name.

Your team, organized

Add loan officers (they price and submit) and processors (they see every deal and upload documents). Only the owner sees compensation.

Know when pricing moves

Emails when SLA's rate sheets change, a daily note on saved quotes whose rate moved, and rate-lock reminders before a lock expires. Turn each one on or off.

How you get paid

You set your broker points (and an optional processing fee) on each deal. Your fee shows on the term sheet and is paid at closing on the settlement statement. Some programs pay through Bill.com after closing instead. Either way, the portal's Compensation page shows every closed deal, what was paid, and your year-to-date and all-time totals, with a downloadable statement.

Questions

Who can join?

Mortgage brokers and brokerages who place business-purpose real estate loans, by invitation from SLA Capital.

Do I need a license?

To be paid a broker fee on a property in Arizona, California, Idaho, Minnesota, Nebraska, Nevada, New York, North Dakota, Oregon or South Dakota, you need a current license for that state. The portal asks for it when you submit there. Elsewhere your NMLS number is optional.

How am I paid?

Your points and any processing fee, paid at closing (or through Bill.com after closing on some programs), all tracked on your Compensation page.

Will SLA contact my borrower around me?

Your borrower stays tied to you for 365 days from your last loan with them.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no fee to join the Preferred Partner program or to use the portal.

Where does SLA lend?

Nationwide except AZ, CA, MN, ND, NV, SD, UT, VT and US territories. Fix & Flip and New Construction also exclude IL, the city of Newark NJ, and rural properties. Multifamily 5+ also excludes ID.
