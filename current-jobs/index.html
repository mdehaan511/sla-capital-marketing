Current openings

Loan Officer — Private LendingRemote (US) · 1099 Independent Contractor · Commission-only, uncapped · $75K–$250K+ first-year OTE

The role

This is a self-production seat: you generate your own business, and you keep the upside. You'll originate business-purpose investor loans — DSCR rentals, Fix & Flip, and New Construction — across the 42 states we lend in. No consumer mortgages, no call-center leads, no cap on what you can earn.

Most commission-only LO jobs hand you a rate sheet and wish you luck. We built something different: the platform, the pricing, and the training are the reason our LOs win deals they'd lose at other shops.

What you get

  • Uncapped commission. Realistic first-year on-target earnings of $75K–$250K+, driven entirely by your production. Top producers exceed it.
  • Rates that win. DSCR from 5.75% on a 30-year fixed, Fix & Flip up to 100% LTC with closings in as little as 72 hours, New Construction at 85% LTC. When you quote, you compete.
  • A custom-built system. Our in-house pricing and origination platform sizes a deal and produces a real term sheet in minutes — while the competition is still promising a callback.
  • Prospecting tools. Purpose-built tools for finding and tracking investors, so building your pipeline is a process, not a cold start.
  • Training. Product, pricing, and sales-process training from day one. You don't need to know private lending yet — you need to be coachable and driven.
  • Speed that closes. Average Fix & Flip close is 7–10 days. Fast execution is the easiest referral engine in this business.

What you'll do

  • Prospect real estate investors, agents, and referral partners to build your own book of business
  • Price deals and deliver term sheets using our in-house sizer
  • Guide borrowers from application through closing, working with our processing team
  • Grow repeat and referral volume by executing fast and shooting straight

Who thrives here

  • Self-starters who want their income tied to their output — this is commission-only, and that's the point
  • Backgrounds in sales, real estate, or finance; lending experience is a plus, not a requirement — we train
  • Comfortable working virtually and running your own day
  • Straight shooters — our brand is "the rate you're quoted is the rate you close at," and our people match it

Details

  • Location: Remote, anywhere in the US (HQ: Spokane, WA)
  • Engagement: 1099 independent contractor
  • Compensation: Commission-only, uncapped — scales with experience and production
  • Start: Immediately

How to apply

Email a resume — or just a note on who you are and how you'd build your book — and we'll set up a conversation.
