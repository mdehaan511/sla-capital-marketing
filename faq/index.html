DSCR rental loans

What is a DSCR loan?

An investor mortgage that qualifies on the property's rental income, not yours. If rent covers the payment — principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA — the loan qualifies. No tax returns, no W-2s, no DTI. Full mechanics: DSCR Loans Explained.

What are the rates?

30-year fixed from 6.97% (before buy down: highest credit tier, DSCR 1.20+), plus ARM and interest-only structures. Always current on the public rate sheet — and the rate you're quoted is the rate you close at.

What credit score do I need?

660 minimum; best pricing at 740+. The 660–679 band closes with pre-approval, 1.10x DSCR, and extra reserves. The full box: DSCR Loan Requirements.

Do I need a lease in place?

No. Vacant properties qualify on the appraiser's market-rent report (Form 1007). That's also how we handle Airbnbs and short-term rentals — long-term market rent is the anchor, and your STR upside stays yours.

How long is cash-out seasoning?

6 months — the industry standard, honored in writing. Conventional lenders make you wait 12: the seasoning math.

DSCR or conventional — which should I use?

Conventional can win on rate if you have clean W-2 income, few properties, and personal title is fine. DSCR wins on everything else: no income docs, no 10-property cap, LLC closings, 6-month seasoning. The honest comparison: DSCR vs. Conventional.

Fix & Flip

How fast can you close?

As little as 72 hours with clear title and a complete file; 7–10 days is the average. Speed is the point — it's what wins competitive deals.

How much leverage do I get?

Up to 92.5% loan-to-cost for experienced flippers, with 100% of the rehab budget funded via draws. First flip? You're welcome here — expect ~85% LTC to start: the first-timer guide.

How is my loan size determined?

Two tests — loan-to-cost and a cap against after-repair value — and your loan is the lesser. How appraisers actually land on ARV: ARV explained.

Hard money vs. DSCR — which do I need?

Hard money buys the project; DSCR holds the property. Flipping or rehabbing → bridge. Renting and holding → DSCR. Doing BRRRR → both, back to back.

New Construction

What does the loan cover?

Land (or lot payoff) plus the vertical build — up to 85% of each — on 18 or 24 month terms, from 10%. Spec builds welcome: how ground-up financing works.

How do draws work?

Budget sits in escrow, releases against completed milestones after app-based photo inspections — funds wire in days, and interest accrues on the drawn balance only. The full cycle: construction draws explained.

Do I need building experience?

Experience earns leverage, but a first ground-up build with a licensed GC, approved plans, a contingency line, and documented liquidity is fundable.
